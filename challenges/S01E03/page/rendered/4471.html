Since April

Lessons

  • Merging by subject (decision 0001) finds reports that are the same wording; it doesn’t check they’re the same bug. Two different bugs can produce the same complaint (“wrong day”) and land in one ticket. Merge by subject to triage fast, then check cause before calling it one issue.
  • Dedupe by id before you count anything — the export reused 15 ids across pages. “412 reports” was never typed here again after that was found; the table above computes it.
  • Write the page while the queue is still in your head, and come back to it when the real breakdown changes — a known ticket that’s actually two tickets is a stale page, not a closed one.
