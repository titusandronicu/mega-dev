## Intent
In the week view, hovering over a booking shows who booked it, next to its time.

## Acceptance
- Given a booking in the week view, when the staff hover over it, then the tooltip shows the time and the name of the person who booked.
- Given a booking with no name, when the staff hover over it, then the tooltip shows the time only and no empty label.
- Given a keyboard user, when they focus a booking, then the same information is available.
- Given the week view, when the tooltip is added, then it must not hide or move the booking or change what is shown today.

## Boundaries
- Must not show this on the public booking page; the week view is for staff.
- Must not change stored data.

Allowed by AUT-1 ("new columns, filters or fields that add to what is there and take nothing away").
