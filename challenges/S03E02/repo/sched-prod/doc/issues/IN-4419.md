## Intent
The client's logo keeps its proportions in the booking confirmation email. The same ticket also reports the "Add to calendar" link opening a blank page: that is a second problem, filed as TRI-4419-calendar.

## Acceptance
- Given a logo that is wider than tall, when the confirmation email is rendered, then it keeps its aspect ratio.
- Given a square or tall logo, when the email is rendered, then it keeps its aspect ratio too.
- Given an account with no logo, when the email is sent, then it is sent as today with no broken image.
- Given the fix, when the email is sent, then it must not change the text, links or layout of the email apart from the logo.

## Boundaries
- Must not touch the "Add to calendar" link (TRI-4419-calendar).
- Must not change the reminder email.

Allowed by AUT-1 ("bug fixes and small features inside the specs, with a test that shows the change").
