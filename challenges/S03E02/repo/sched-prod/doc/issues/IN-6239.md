## Question
Support (IN-6239) asks for 6-character booking references instead of 10, because people mistype them on the phone. A client (IN-6249) says their front-desk software matches the 10-character references and asks us not to shorten them. Do we shorten them, and what happens to the existing ones?

## Options
1. Keep 10 characters for everyone, but make them easier to say and type (no look-alike letters, shown in groups, case-insensitive).
2. New bookings get 6-character references; old ones stay as they are.
3. Rewrite every stored reference to 6 characters.

## Recommendation
Option 1.

## Why
Because IN-6249 is a client telling us they rely on the 10-character references, and AUT-2 says a change to something a client relies on needs a person; option 3 would also rewrite stored data, which AUT-2 reserves for a person. Option 1 answers the problem in IN-6239 (mistyping on the phone) and breaks nobody.
