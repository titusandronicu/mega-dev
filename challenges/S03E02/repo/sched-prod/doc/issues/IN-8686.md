## Question
Support (IN-8686) says EU accounts want the dates in the CSV export as dd/mm, "a one-line change". A client (IN-1332, a studio in Auckland) says their payroll import reads the export as mm/dd today and asks us not to flip it. Which date order does the export use, and for whom?

## Options
1. Keep mm/dd for everyone; tell the EU accounts it stays.
2. A per-account export setting for the date order, with mm/dd as the default so nothing changes for accounts that did not choose.
3. Change the export to day first for EU accounts, and warn the clients who read it today.

## Recommendation
Option 2.

## Why
Because IN-1332 is a client telling us they rely on the current order, and AUT-2 says a change to something a client relies on needs a person. Option 2 gives the EU accounts their order and breaks nobody; PRD-3 also says an export never loses what it had.
